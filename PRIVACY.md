# TabJury Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

TabJury is a browser extension that manages your open tabs. It does not collect, sell, or
share your data. TabJury has no servers, and it sends nothing to the developer or to anyone
else.

## What TabJury reads

To do its job, TabJury reads information about your open tabs: their web addresses (URLs),
titles, icons, and when you last used them. It uses this only to find duplicate and unused
tabs, switch to a tab that is already open, and put tabs to sleep or close them according to
your settings. This information is processed on your device and never leaves it.

## What TabJury stores

TabJury saves the following using your browser's built-in extension storage:

- **Your settings**, including the list of websites you choose to protect. These are saved
  in your browser's sync storage. If you have browser sync turned on, your browser (for
  example Chrome with your Google account) copies them to your other devices. TabJury does
  not control or access that sync.
- **Recently closed**: the address, title, and icon of tabs TabJury closed, so you can
  restore them. Kept on your device for 14 days by default, up to 500 entries.
- **History**: the address and title of the last 200 tabs TabJury closed or put to sleep
  automatically. Kept on your device only.
- **Temporary state**, such as the pause timer, which is cleared when the browser closes.

You can clear Recently closed and History at any time from the TabJury popup. Uninstalling
TabJury removes all of its stored data from your browser.

## What TabJury does not do

- It does not send any data over the network.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, ads, or cookies.
- It does not load or run code from the internet.
- It does not sell or transfer your data to third parties.

## Permissions

- **tabs**: read tab addresses, titles, and last-used times to find duplicate and unused tabs.
- **storage**: save your settings, Recently closed, and History.
- **alarms**: check your tabs every 30 seconds and time automatic cleanup.
- **sessions**: restore tabs from Recently closed.
- **favicon**: show website icons in the popup.
